Soil conditioners and manure

Aarambh

Organic soil conditioner

High-quality organic soil conditioner for all crops. 100% organic and eco-friendly.

  • Improves soil structure, aeration and moisture retention
  • Enriches soil with nutrients and beneficial microorganisms
  • Promotes vigorous roots and better nutrient uptake
Form
Powder / bag

Enriched Organic Manure

Crop-specific range

A range of crop-specific enriched organic manures: Sugarcane, Cotton, Maize, Soyabean, Cereals and Onion Special.

  • Improves soil fertility and organic carbon content
  • Supplies macro and micronutrients in balanced form
  • Reduces dependence on chemical fertilisers over time
Form
Bag

Granules for soil application

Gloster GR

Organic granule, 20 kg

Granular organic input for vegetable, field and fruit crops.

  • Improves root growth and nutrient uptake
  • Boosts chlorophyll and photosynthesis
  • Helps plants tolerate drought and stress
Dose
Vegetable crops: 25 kg per acre. Field crops: 25 to 50 kg per acre. Fruit crops: 1 to 2 kg per plant.

Rootwell GR

Phosphate granule

Soil-applied granule that makes natural phosphate readily available. Suitable for all crops.

  • Strong root development and better nutrient uptake
  • Improves efficiency of other fertilisers
  • Better flowering, fruit filling, colour and shelf life
Dose
Sugarcane, groundnut, fruit and flower crops: 10 to 25 kg per acre. Vegetables: 10 to 25 kg at planting and again after one month. Foliar: 1 to 2 gm per litre.

Foliar and drip powders

Max-O Potash

Organic potash from marine red algae

Natural potash derived from Rhodophytes for fruits, vegetables and field crops.

  • Improves flowering and fruit setting
  • Enhances fruit size, colour, sweetness and shine
  • Increases resistance to drought, heat and stress
Dose
Foliar spray: 0.5 to 1 gm per litre of water.

Unnati Silicon Star

Silicon fertiliser

Strengthens plant cell walls and improves stem strength.

  • Reduces lodging in cereals and tall crops
  • Better resistance to pests, disease and fungal attack
  • Improves tolerance to drought, heat and salinity
Dose
Foliar spray: 0.5 to 1 gm per litre of water.

Unnati Boron (Powder)

Di-sodium octaborate, 20% B min.

Micronutrient fertiliser that corrects boron deficiency in all crops.

  • Improves flowering and pollen viability
  • Reduces flower and fruit drop
  • Reduces cracking and internal fruit disorders
Dose
Foliar spray: 1 to 2 gm per litre. Drip or soil: 500 gm to 1 kg per acre.

Unnati Chelated Zinc 12%

Zn-EDTA

Chelated zinc for fast absorption and quick correction of zinc deficiency.

  • Promotes chlorophyll formation and greener leaves
  • Enhances root growth, shoots and branching
  • Supports flowering, fruit setting and grain quality
Dose
Foliar spray: 0.5 to 1 gm per litre of water.

Unnati liquid fertilisers

Unnati Calmag

NK 6:0:18 with calcium, magnesium and boron

Suspension fertiliser for vegetables, fruits and field crops.

  • Better flowering, fruit setting and less flower drop
  • Calcium improves firmness and shelf life
  • Magnesium supports chlorophyll and photosynthesis
Dose
Foliar spray: 2 ml per litre. Drip irrigation: 1 litre per acre.

Unnati Zinbor

NPK 11:11:8 with zinc and boron

Balanced NPK suspension fortified with zinc and boron.

  • Strong roots and vigorous vegetative growth
  • Zinc corrects deficiency and improves enzyme activity
  • Boron aids flowering, pollen viability and fruit setting
Dose
Foliar spray: 2 ml per litre. Drip irrigation: 1 litre per acre.

Unnati Zinc Gluconate

Chelated zinc as zinc glycine liquid

Quickly corrects zinc deficiency in crops.

  • Improves chlorophyll formation and green foliage
  • Enhances enzyme activity and metabolism
  • Supports flowering, fruit setting and crop quality
Dose
Foliar spray: 1 to 2 ml per litre. Drip or soil: 500 ml to 1 litre per acre.

Unnati Boron (Liquid)

Chelated boron as boron glycine liquid

Quickly corrects boron deficiency in crops.

  • Enhances flowering and pollen viability
  • Improves fruit setting, reduces flower and fruit drop
  • Increases size, shine and shelf life of produce
Dose
Foliar spray: 1 to 2 ml per litre of water.

Unnati Calcium

Concentrated liquid calcium 11%

Prevents and corrects calcium deficiency in crops.

  • Strengthens cell walls and plant structure
  • Reduces blossom end rot, cracking and internal disorders
  • Increases shelf life, size and quality of produce
Dose
Foliar spray: 2 to 3 ml per litre of water.

Boosters and spray aids

Energy

Booster for every plant

Plant booster for flowering, fruit setting and quality.

  • Induces female flowers, helping fruit set
  • Improves quality of fruits and vegetables
  • Increases chlorophyll and photosynthesis
Dose
2 to 2.5 ml per litre of water.

S-Ortho Plus

Ortho silicic acid OSA 2.0% WSL

Beneficial-element fertiliser that strengthens the plant.

  • Strengthens cell walls and stem strength
  • Better resistance to pests, disease and stress
  • Reduces lodging in cereals and tall crops
Dose
Foliar spray: 1 to 2 ml per litre of water.

PH Balancer

pH balancer for spray solution

Corrects spray water pH so crop inputs work better.

  • Prevents alkaline hydrolysis, increases spray performance
  • Improves effectiveness of pesticides, fungicides and fertilisers
  • Reduces hard water effect and improves mixing
Dose
0.5 to 1 ml per litre of water for a normal spray solution.
